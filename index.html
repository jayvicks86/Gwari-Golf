Counties League

Enter once. Play twice.

Opt in and pick the county you'll represent. Every round you log counts twice: up your handicap-flighted ladder, and into your county's average against every other county, all season long.

  1. 1Enter, and get a county suggested from where you play
  2. 2Log rounds through the season, same as always
  3. 3Climb your flight ladder, and your county's table

Trade Wind Shield

Europe versus North America. Every round counts.

Once a year, the whole of Gwari splits in two: Europe on one side, the North America on the other. You're entered automatically, and every round you log that week adds to your side's tally. Only one shield gets lifted.
