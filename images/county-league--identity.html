Badges, not ghosts

A direction for the League / Shield register. The ghost is the mascot of the casual, selectable modes (Daily, Duel, Hunt). The seasonal institutions — County League, Trade Wind Shield — speak heraldry instead: crests, monograms, laurels, gold. Here’s the reworked champion crest, plus two ways to identify players on the ladder.

Two registers, deliberately different. Ghost family = pick-up modes you schedule like a round or a mini-game; playful; ghost-token identity. Heraldic family = always-on, allegiance-based seasons your rounds feed into; serious; badge identity. The Shield already omitted ghosts — this just makes that the rule for the whole tier.
Why a badge, not the hero ghost. The hero mark’s ghost is entangled with the flag/pole and never shrank cleanly to a token — that limitation is precisely the case for a purpose-built badge in this tier. The ghost keeps its job as the brand watermark on cards and the face of the casual modes; it just stops being the per-player chip in League/Shield.
Same navy/gold system. The move is a clean split: ghost = casual/selectable (Daily, Duel, Hunt), heraldry = serious/seasonal (League, Shield). Crest is a champion-gold heater shield with a navy chief; ladder identity becomes a monogram badge with a gold leader.
