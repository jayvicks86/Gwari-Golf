Counties

The opt-in, season-long league that runs all year. Enter once, pick the county you’ll represent, and every round you log counts two ways — up your handicap-flighted ladder, and into your county’s average against every other county. Two competitions, one entry, one ghost.

Opt-in, not auto. Unlike the Trade Wind Shield (which enters everyone), the County League is a deliberate Enter. On entry you’re suggested a county from your home club + where you play, but the choice is yours — and it locks for the season so nobody bandwagons mid-year. These screens are layout only; the model itself is already locked in the worklog (2026-06-14 regional entry + the Phase-0 prompt).
Two competitions from one entry. (a) Your ladder — your rank within your county, flighted by handicap band so a 20-handicapper races other 20s and can actually win their flight. (b) County vs county — your rounds roll up into your county’s average (never a total: a min-qualifier threshold means a small keen county like Cornwall can top a big one). The two live behind one My Ladder / Counties toggle.
Sharing mirrors the Shield. The collective winner is announced by the official Gwari accounts; individuals get the personal cards — monthly feats through the season, and a winner’s card at the end for members of the champion county showing their own contribution. Per-user cards stay tied to individual achievement, never the collective result — protects the feed and the moat.
Identity: heraldry, not ghosts. The ghost is the mascot of the casual modes (Daily, Duel, Hunt); the serious tier drops it. Players are a monogram roundel (cap-badge; gold = leader). Regions get a crest from a scaling system — frame + tincture (± division) + optional charge — real emblem for marquee counties (Cornwall’s St Piran cross), generated default for the rest. See regional-crest-system.html.
Same navy/gold system as the Trade Wind Shield set. Player identity on the ladder is a colour-free monogram roundel (gold = leader); region identity is a crest from the scaling heraldic system (frame + tincture ± division + optional charge), champion-gold for winners — so gold = winning runs unbroken from roundel to crest. Scoring is Net Stableford, averaged; county-vs-county uses a min-qualifier threshold so a small keen county can top a big one. Two competitions, one opt-in entry, season-locked allegiance. The ghost stays as the brand watermark and the face of the casual modes.
