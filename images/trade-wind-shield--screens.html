Trade Wind Shield

The once-a-year week where all of Gwari splits in two — Fescue (Europe) versus Bermuda (USA). Auto-entered for everyone: your logged rounds just start counting. The flow is a home-page card → an explainer you can read and close → the live scoreboard.

Auto-enter vs opt-in. The Shield enters everyone automatically and simply registers the rounds you log during the week — if you’re not interested you never have to open the card, and nothing about your normal round-logging changes. The Counties League (shown beneath it on the home screen) is the opposite: a deliberate opt-in you Enter.
One design choice to confirm — daily sessions. To earn the Ryder-Cup scoreboard feel, I’ve framed the week as seven daily sessions: each day goes to whichever side has the better average, and you win the Shield by winning the most days. It’s far more dramatic than a single week-long average (which trends to a dead heat near ~36 pts). The raw week-long average is still shown as a secondary readout, so it stays faithful to “just register rounds.” Happy to drop sessions back to a pure cumulative average if you’d rather.
Static SVG, same 360×640 phone frame and navy/gold system as the other screens. Team palette is turf-coded — Fescue cool links green, Bermuda warm clay — cool vs warm like the Ryder Cup’s blue vs red, with Heritage Gold reserved for the leading side and the Shield itself. Ghosts intentionally left out.
